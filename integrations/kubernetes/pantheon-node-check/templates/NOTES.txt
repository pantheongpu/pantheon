One Job per node in .Values.nodes. Watch them with:

  kubectl get jobs -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
  kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} --tail=20

A Job that completed means every workload passed on every card of that node.
A Job that failed means a workload failed on a card; its log says which.
